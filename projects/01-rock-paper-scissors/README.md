# ⭐ Rock, Paper, Scissors
**Do this after:** Module 02

## Requirements
- The player types `rock`, `paper` or `scissors` (or `r`/`p`/`s`)
- The computer chooses randomly (`random.choice`)
- Print both choices and who won the round
- Keep score; play until the player types `quit`
- Show the final score at the end
